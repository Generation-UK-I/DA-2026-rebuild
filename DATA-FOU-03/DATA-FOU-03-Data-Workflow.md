Data Analysis Process:
- Asking the Question: Clearly outline the question or issue you need to address.
- Getting the Data: Gather the necessary information from relevant sources.
- Investigating the Data: Examine the data to understand its content and quality.
- Preparing the Data: Clean and organise the data to make it ready for analysis.
- Analysing the Data: Use analytical techniques to explore and interpret the data.
- Presenting the Results: Share the findings and insights in a clear and actionable manner.
